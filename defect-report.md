# Sample Defect Report

**Defect ID:** BUG-001  
**Title:** Basket total does not refresh after changing product quantity  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** High  
**Environment:** Chrome / Windows 11  

## Steps to reproduce
1. Log in to the application.
2. Add a product to the basket.
3. Open the basket.
4. Increase the product quantity.
5. Observe the displayed total.

## Expected result
The basket total should immediately reflect the updated quantity.

## Actual result
The quantity changes but the displayed total remains unchanged until the page is refreshed.

## Evidence to capture
Screenshot, browser version, test data and timestamp.
